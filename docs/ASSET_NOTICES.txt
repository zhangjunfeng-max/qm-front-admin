APPLICATION ASSET SOURCES

Project-created defaults: participant silhouettes (CSS only), platform geometric
logo/favicon/status illustrations and regenerated geometric test posters. No
stock human photography, external cartoon characters, portrait API or external
artwork is used for these defaults. Asset construction is recorded in the
backend scripts/replace-demo-assets.py; the application's grant is not extended
to customer uploads.

The mobile activity-demo.m4a is an original synthesized 19.2-second demonstration
melody made from sine-wave harmonics without sampled recordings. It is used as
a development fallback only; production activities play their configured music.
Preview MP4 clips are synthetic development fixtures, not third-party footage.
